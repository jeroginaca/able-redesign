02 Why it matters

After a spinal cord injury, standing up is not a metaphor. It means being at eye level again, a hug that doesn't come from below, and a first step in months. A few weeks later it means the thousandth.

    04 The device

    Built for the gym floor,
    not the showroom.

    Everything a therapist needs to get a patient standing, and nothing to wrestle with between sessions.

    • 9.8kgDevice weight
    • 150–190cmUser height
    • ≤100kgUser weight
    • C5–L5Injury levels
    • <7minTo fit
    1. 01

      Powered knee

      A motorised joint drives knee flexion and extension through the step.

    2. 02

      Passive hip

      The hip joint is unpowered, so the pelvis moves naturally and the device stays light.

    3. 03

      Adjustable uprights

      Thigh and shank segments adjust to fit users from 150 to 190 cm.

    4. 04

      Quick-fit cuffs

      Fitting and removal averaged 6:50 minutes across the multicentre trial.

    5. 05

      Crutch-assisted balance

      Users walk with forearm crutches. Elbow extensors need at least 4/5 strength in both arms.
